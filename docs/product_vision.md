# Project Name: GeoScan Attendance Pro

## Problem Statement
- Taking attendance by hand takes too much ime nad it is easy to make mistakes. It is also easy for studebnts to cheat by signing the register for friends who are not actually in class.Lecturers do noy have a fast automatic way to check who is reallu there without stopping their lesson.

## Target Users 
- **Students:** Users who need a quick and an advanced way to register their presence
- **Lecturers:** These who nees a quick, accurate and automatic list of who were present in class
- **University Administrators:** Those who require accurate learner analytics for progress tracking    and reporting

## Unique Value Proposition
- This attendance system prevent cheating by using QR codes that change and expire quickly along with GPS location tracking. Students can only mark attendance if they are in the right place at the right time during the lecture. It saves time and provide accurate resulys

## Key Features
- **Dynamic QR Generation**
- **GPS-Location Locking**
- **Real-time Attendance Logs**
- **Automated Session Expiry**

## Success Metrics
- Attendance Accuracy
- Time Efficiency
- User Adoption

## Moore'se Vision Statement 

- **FOR:** Students, Lectures, and Educational Institutions.
- **WHO:** Struggle with not receiving accurate attendance data because of friends signing for others 
- **THE:** GEOSCAN ATTENDANCE PRO
- **IS A:** Location-based attendance system
- **THAT:** Guarentees attendance is accurate by requiring a physical scan within a specific GPS radius using a code that expires after each class.
- **UNLIKE:** Traditional or Manual paper registers that can be signed by onee person for five people
- **OUR PRODUCT:** Provides a secure, automated and GPS verified solution that ensure only students physically in the room can be marked present, improving data accuracy and saving time for lessons.
