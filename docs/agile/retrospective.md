Sprint 1 Retrospective

What Went Well

* The implementation of the QR code scanning feature was successful.
* Location-based validation performed as anticipated.
* Throughout the sprint, there was constant team communication.
* Tasks were monitored and updated on a regular basis.

What Could Be Improved

* In certain instances, attendance marking was impacted by GPS accuracy problems.
* Delays in front-end and back-end component integration.
* Certain tasks took longer than anticipated because they were underestimated.

Action Items

* Investigate enhanced location management (e.g., Wi-Fi or hybrid location approaches).
* Divide work into smaller, more doable chunks.
* Enhance integration testing at the beginning of the sprint.
* Plan mid-sprint check-ins to monitor progress.
