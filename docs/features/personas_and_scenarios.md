# User Personas and Scenarios
## Persona 1: Dr Mkhize (Lecturer )
- **Age**: 45
- **Occupation**: University Lecturer
- **Goal**: Record student attendance quickly and accurately

## Scenario
Dr. Mkhize starts his lecture and generates a QR code using the system. He displays the QR code to students in class. Students scan the code, and the system verifies their location before marking them present. After the lecture, Dr. Mkhize views the attendance records on the system.

## Persona 2: Aphiwe(Student)
- **Age**: 21
- **Occupation** University Student
- **Goal**: Mark attendance in an advanced way and ensure it is recorded

## Scenario
Aphiwe arrives at class and opens the attendance system on her phone. She scans the QR code displayed by the lecturer. The system confirms her location and marks her attendance instantly. She sees a confirmation that her attendance has been successfully recorded.

## Persona 3: Mr. Mathew 
- **Age**: 37
- **Occupation**: University Administrator
- **Goal**: Access accurate learner analytics for progress tracking and reporting

## Scenario
Mr. Mathew regularly logs into the system to check how students are attending classes across the institution. He reviews attendance reports, looks for patterns such as low attendance in certain courses, and uses this information to support academic planning. Having reliable data helps him make informed decisions and ensures the university’s records are accurate and up to date.
